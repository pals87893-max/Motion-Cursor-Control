📱 Phone Controller

Use your phone to control your laptop's mouse.

Type the number shown on your laptop program.

Need help finding the number?
  1. Make sure your phone and laptop use the same Wi-Fi.
  2. On your laptop, open Command Prompt (search for "cmd" in the Start menu).
  3. Type ipconfig and press Enter.
  4. Find the line called IPv4 Address. The number next to it is what you type above.
  5. Start the controller program on your laptop before tapping Connect.
